The ANIVERA lifecycle

From first record to
the next chapter.

Keep important context connected as an animal's needs, care team, and environment change.

01 / REGISTER

Create a trusted profile

Record identity, species, origin, identifiers, and essential background.

02 / CARE

Coordinate daily care

Organize health events, treatment notes, tasks, and upcoming reminders.

03 / CONNECT

Keep history together

Document authorized custody changes, handovers, and care-team updates.

04 / CONTINUE

Record every outcome

Maintain a lifecycle timeline through adoption, transfer, release, or later-life care.
