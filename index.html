Platform Architecture

How ANIVERA Works

ANIVERA connects verified taxonomic reference profiles with longitudinal individual animal care records across four operational stages.

01

Verify Taxonomic Identity

Anchor every animal record to a validated non-avian species profile with scientific nomenclature, habitat baselines, and biometric or tag identifiers.

02

Log Clinical & Health History

Maintain chronological veterinary exams, immunizations, water or enclosure parameters, diagnostics, and treatment protocols in one structured ledger.

03

Execute Care Routines & Tasks

Schedule species-appropriate nutrition rations, habitat maintenance, enrichment, and preventive tasks with due dates and completion tracking.

04

Track Lifecycle Milestones

Document every developmental transition—from birth, hatching, or metamorphosis through juvenile growth, adult care, and senior management.

Connected Lifecycle Modules

Built for Every Stage of Non-Avian Animal Care

From conservation biologists tracking wild megafauna to agricultural managers, marine sanctuaries, herpetologists, and companion caretakers.

01 • Identity & Profiles

Animal Profiles and Identification

Standardized identity records adapt to the identification method appropriate for each taxonomic group:

  • Wild Mammals: Camera-trap coat pattern biometrics, ear notches, and GPS/VHF collar IDs.
  • Livestock & Companion: ISO 11784/11785 microchips, RFID/EID ear tags, and pedigree registries.
  • Marine & Aquatic: Ventral fluke photography, dorsal fin notch catalogs, PIT tags, and broodstock IDs.
  • Reptiles, Amphibians & Invertebrates: Carapace scute codes, morphotype patterns, and instar/molt logs.
02 • Clinical Ledger

Health and Care History

Capture species-relevant clinical observations without forcing disparate animals into a single rigid clinic template:

  • Preventive Medicine: Core vaccinations, parasite FAMACHA/fecal scoring, and quarantine PCR screens.
  • Environmental & Water Quality: Salinity, dissolved oxygen, pH, UVB Ferguson zones, and humidity gradients.
  • Dental & Morphological Checks: Elephant molar progression, equine floating, elodont incisor wear, and ecdysis completeness.
  • Diagnostic Continuity: Full chronological history preserved across transfers between sanctuaries, pastures, or households.
03 • Operational Protocols

Care Routines and Tasks

Translate biological requirements into daily, weekly, and seasonal care tasks with status tracking and due dates:

  • Nutrition & Foraging: Ruminant silage ratios, obligate carnivore taurine balances, gut-loaded insect schedules, and browse provisioning.
  • Habitat & Husbandry: Rotational grazing moves, biofilter testing, reverse-osmosis misting, and burrow substrate renewal.
  • Behavioral Enrichment: Extractive foraging puzzles, pack social rank observation, and thermal basking gradients.
04 • Developmental Timeline

Lifecycle Milestones

Monitor critical biological transitions tailored to each animal's life history strategy:

  • Neonatal & Early Stage: Colostrum intake, marsupial pouch emergence, larval instars, and amphibian metamorphosis.
  • Juvenile & Subadult: Weaning weights, smoltification, territory dispersal, and forest-school climbing milestones.
  • Adult & Senior Care: Reproductive estrus/calving logs, annual shearing or brumation cycles, and geriatric mobility support.
